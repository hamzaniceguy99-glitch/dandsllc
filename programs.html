Four services under one written agreement, for residential and small commercial pools.

Program 01 · The same day each week

Weekly pool service

A fixed service day means you know when we've been and can see the result. Every visit covers the physical cleaning and the baskets, and you get a short note — what was done, what the water tested at, and anything that needs attention.

  • Residential and commercial
  • Monthly fee in writing
  • Gate and pool access

Program 02 · Measured, not guessed

Testing & chemical balance

Chemistry drifts with weather, bather load and rainfall, so dosing on a schedule rather than on readings wastes chemicals and still lets the water turn. We test at every visit, adjust to the numbers, and log the readings so a pattern is visible over weeks.

  • Every visit
  • All pools
  • Included in service
  • Chemicals per label

Program 03 · Before it fails

Equipment checks & minor repairs

Equipment gives warning before it fails — noise, pressure, run time. We check it on every visit, clean or backwash filters on schedule, and handle minor repairs. Major equipment replacement, electrical work and structural repair go to licensed contractors, and we'll tell you plainly when that's the case.

  • Every visit
  • All pools
  • Minor repairs quoted
  • Licensed work referred

Program 04 · Before and after

Seasonal & storm care

Florida weather is the main variable. We prepare pools ahead of a storm where there's time, and handle the recovery afterwards — debris, chemistry and equipment — which is when most pools are lost to algae for a fortnight.
